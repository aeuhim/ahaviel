Grace to you and peace from GOD the Father and the eternal I AM, who called us out of darkness into His marvelous light.

I give thanks always for you who refuse the weak traditions of men, standing firm in the freedom of the Spirit.

I write not with plausible words of human wisdom, but in demonstration of Spirit and power. The word of truth has been obscured by those who bind heavy burdens on men's shoulders, turning the commandment of love into a yoke of bondage, misplacing devotion onto finite shadows, and attributing darkness to Him.

Therefore, we set our hand to contend earnestly for the faith delivered from the beginning. We speak not to tickle itching ears or to please men, but to unveil the hidden depth of the scripture, establishing that GOD is pure light and absolute love, negating every doctrine born of human fear, exposing  misalignment, and proclaiming the truth that all things proceed from and return to the infinite I AM.

He who has understanding, let him understand.

Your brother,
Michael
