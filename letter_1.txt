The Illusion of Darkness: Why Light is the Only Substance

GOD is pure light, and GOD is absolute love. These are not mere attributes He chooses to display, but the very essence of His uncreated nature. The apostle John writes in 1 John 1:5, "This is the message we have heard from Him and announce to you, that GOD is Light, and in Him there is no darkness at all." He reinforces this foundational truth in 1 John 4:8, plainly declaring that "GOD is love." In 1 John 4:16, scripture seals this non-negotiable reality, "GOD is love, and the one who abides in love abides in GOD, and GOD abides in him." If scripture explicitly establishes that GOD is light and love, then anything attempting to attribute absolute darkness, arbitrary wrath, or an unending, irredeemable void to Him is an illusion born of human legalism, limited sight, and fear.

To understand why darkness, judgment, and evil are not competing forces against GOD, we must look at how scripture defines His all-encompassing presence and sovereignty. Paul writes in Colossians 1:17 that "He is before all things, and in Him all things hold together." In Acts 17:28, he proclaims to the thinkers of Athens, "For in Him we live and move and exist." Furthermore, Paul looks toward the ultimate unveiling in 1 Corinthians 15:28, declaring that the divine destiny of creation is for "GOD to be all in all." If GOD fills all things, holds all things together, and is ultimately all in all, then an absolute void or an independent, permanent kingdom of darkness existing outside of Him is an ontological impossibility.

How then does darkness appear to rule the human mind, and why do men claim they can permanently resist the Almighty? Through the creaturely illusion of absolute autonomy. Scripture makes it undeniable that human beings do not possess an independent "free will" capable of permanently thwarting the Creator's grand design. We do not control our own heartbeats, our autonomic systems, or the fundamental laws of existence that sustain us. Paul explicitly affirms this in Romans 9:16, writing, "So then it depends not on human will or exertion, but on GOD, who has mercy." Far from being an unintended error, scripture reveals that human weakness, blindness, and bondage were allowed as a temporary, necessary phase to manifest the full magnitude of divine grace. In Romans 11:32, Paul exposes the master strategy, "For GOD has bound everyone over to disobedience so that He may have mercy on them all."

Because human autonomy is a finite illusion and GOD's nature is restorative love, divine judgment is not a mindless, endless torture applied from the outside by an angry deity. Scripture shows that the fire of judgment is an internal, refining friction, the inescapable ignition of truth when uncreated light hits a heart and mind operating in delusion and foolishness. In Ezekiel 28:18, scripture reveals the origin of this burn, "Therefore I brought fire from within you; it has consumed you." Isaiah 33:11 confirms that a person's own corrupted breath becomes the flame: "Your breath is a fire that consumes you." When judgment falls, it is the agony of true realization, the painful stripping away of the ego as the soul is forced to see the full spectrum of its own foolishness in the brilliant light of reality.

This internal fire is surgical, educational, and remedial. In Isaiah 26:9, scripture declares the explicit purpose of divine severity: "For when Your judgments are in the earth, the inhabitants of the world will learn righteousness." Paul describes this exact purging in 1 Corinthians 3:13-15, where a person's deceptive, foolish works are burned away: "If any man's work is burned up, he will suffer loss; but he himself will be saved, yet so as through fire." The original Greek text confirms this restorative scope, the phrase aionios kolasis in Matthew 25:46 does not speak of endless retributive torture, but rather kolasis, the pruning back of a tree for its future growth, pertaining to the aion, the specific age of correction to come.

This is why the psalmist David saw through the illusion of dualism centuries before modern science understood the mechanics of physical reality. In Psalm 139:11-12, David writes, "If I say, 'Surely the darkness will overwhelm me, and the light around me will be night,' even the darkness is not dark to You, and the night is as bright as the day. Darkness and light are alike to You." To the Uncreated Source, darkness does not exist as a separate entity. What humans perceive as darkness or destructive judgment is merely a localized, temporary privation of awareness, a painful, internal friction as alignment is forcibly restored, rather than an opposing, eternal realm.

This absolute biblical truth is mirrored precisely in the physical world He engineered. People often try to separate science from GOD, but science is simply humanity discovering the mechanics of divine engineering as we come to maturity. In physics, cold is not an independent physical force that fights against heat, coldness is simply the relative absence or lower kinetic state of thermal energy. You can achieve infinite heat, but coldness has an absolute lower boundary. The exact same law governs light and darkness. Darkness has no mass, no frequency, no velocity, and no source. Darkness cannot be generated; it can only occur when light is obscured.

At the quantum level, there is no such thing as absolute zero light in the universe. In quantum electrodynamics, every atom of matter contains and constantly emits thermal photons across the electromagnetic spectrum. Even inside a reinforced concrete bunker buried deep underground where human eyes perceive total pitch blackness, thermal infrared light continues to radiate through the room. A bunker is never devoid of light, it simply lacks the specific narrow frequency that human biological eyes are wired to register. The light is present, but a human lacks the sensory equipment to perceive it. Anything brighter than a given light source makes that source appear as a shadow by comparison, yet it remains light nonetheless.

The same principle applies to human actions and moral choices. There is no such thing as an absolute, independent substance called "evil" that exists on its own. Every person who strays into misalignment does so because they are operating in a state of spiritual blindness, foolishly seeking a perceived good while lacking the capacity to see the truth. A thief does not steal because he loves the abstract concept of crime, he steals to obtain provision or security, which are distorted attempts to secure good. A cheater lies or betrays to avoid pain or preserve a personal benefit, seeking to maintain what they foolishly think is good for themselves. A violent person acts out of a broken desire to gain pleasure or control. They are pursuing a misaligned shadow of good, rather than alignment with absolute goodness itself.

Human judgment fails because human perception sees only localized fragments rather than the full reality. Consider a story where a man flies all the way to the Philippines to take a blade and cut open a young woman's breast. If you hear only that fragment of the story, your immediate reaction is to condemn the act as horrific and evil. But when the full narrative is unveiled, that a surgeon flew across the world to cut into her breast to extract a life threatening tumor and save her life, the entire picture transforms. The physical action remained identical, but the complete context revealed that the intent and outcome were rooted in saving a life. Human legalism judges localized, painful friction as absolute evil because it cannot see the infinite, surgical mechanics of the Source working all things together to burn away the tumor of sin and restore the whole patient.

Just as physics proves you cannot achieve an absolute zero devoid of all energy but can expand into infinite heat and light, scripture proves that darkness and judgment are temporary shadows and internal refiners that must eventually yield to the Uncreated Source. Science and scripture speak the exact same language, the Source is infinite, human foolishness is temporary, and the shadow is bounded.

He who has understanding, let him understand.
